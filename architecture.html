Architectural Invariants & Security Standards

How LAN Innovations guarantees zero data loss, sub-second latency, and seamless S/4HANA upgradeability.

01

Zero-Trust Field Edge

AES-256 SQLCipher encrypted offline databases on field devices. Zero plain-text caching of corporate asset rosters or schematics. Automated biometrics & MDM remote-wipe integration.

• FIPS 140-2 Validated
02

Reverse-Invoke mTLS

SAP Cloud Connector maintains an outbound-only reverse-invoke TLS 1.3 tunnel from on-premise DMZ to SAP BTP. Zero inbound firewall ports opened to the public internet.

• Principal Propagation
03

Clean Core Extensibility

Zero custom modifications in SAP standard namespaces (no modifications in Z-tables inside core ALM/PM). All enhancements decoupled via SAP CAP and standard released BTP APIs.

• Upgrade Safe 100%
04

Autonomous AI Supervisor

Asynchronous agentic listener intercepts concurrency lockouts and delta sync race conditions. Generates deterministic backoff tokens to commit payloads without technician downtime.

• < 2.4s Auto-Remediation
