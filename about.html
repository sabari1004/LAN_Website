Foundational Principles

The 4 LAN Engineering Tenets

01

Clean Core Invariant

The digital core of S/4HANA must remain pristine. We reject short-sighted modifications in standard SAP namespaces. Every extension, custom service, and business logic pipeline is engineered side-by-side on SAP BTP using CAP and ABAP RAP, ensuring zero friction during quarterly SAP upgrade cycles.

SAP BTP Kyma Integration Suite Decoupled APIs
02

100% Offline-First Field Resilience

Field technicians operate where cellular coverage fails. We treat intermittent connectivity as the default operating environment, not an edge case. Using AES-256 encrypted SQLite stores, granular MAIF delta tokens, and deterministic conflict matrices, we guarantee zero lost inspection records.

SSAM 2405 MDK Client SQLCipher AES-256
03

Deterministic AI Over Theoretical Hype

We build governed, state-machine verified agentic pipelines that solve measurable enterprise problems. From auto-remediating OData lock collisions to predicting equipment parts failure, our AI integrations use the open Model Context Protocol (MCP) with full audit trails and zero hallucination risk.

Model Context Protocol Self-Healing Sync OpenTelemetry
04

Vibe Coding & Spec-Driven Velocity

We embrace AI-assisted pair programming and spec-driven development using Google Antigravity IDE and modern toolchains. By eliminating boilerplate and automating test verification, our senior pods deliver production-grade enterprise software 3x to 5x faster than legacy consultancies.

Antigravity IDE Fastify & Next.js 10-Day Prototypes
