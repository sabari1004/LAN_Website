SAP Pillar 03 • Operational Safety & Compliance

SAP Environment, Health, and Safety (EHSM) Operations

Incident Management, Operational Risk Assessment, Digital Permit to Work (PTW) & Environmental Emissions.

Consult EHSM Architects

Heavy asset industries operate under strict safety mandates where untracked hazards, delayed incident notifications, and manual paper permits create severe operational risks and legal liabilities. We architect and implement modern SAP Environment, Health, and Safety Management (EHSM) integrated seamlessly into S/4HANA Plant Maintenance and field execution. By coupling automated OSHA compliance and multi-tier risk matrices with electronic Permit to Work (PTW) and mobile checklists, our solutions protect frontline field technicians, streamline root-cause investigations, and enforce zero-tolerance safety interlocks before work orders begin.

Core Engineering Deliverables

1. Incident Management & Regulatory Compliance (OSHA / RIDDOR) Zero-Tolerance Safety

Streamlined capture of injuries, illnesses, near-misses, and asset safety observations. Automated root-cause investigation workflows (5-Why, TapRooT, Fishbone), corrective action tracking (CAPA), and automated generation of OSHA 300, 300A, and 301 compliance filings.

OSHA 300/301 Automation 5-Why Root-Cause Trees CAPA Lifecycle Tracking
2. Operational Risk Assessment & Hierarchy of Controls Hazard Scoring

Multi-factor risk assessment matrices evaluating likelihood, severity, and exposure frequency. Embeds risk ratings directly onto S/4HANA functional locations and equipment, enforcing the industrial hierarchy of controls (Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Admin, PPE).

Risk Matrix Engine Hierarchy of Controls Asset Hazard Profiles
3. Electronic Permit to Work (PTW) & Safe Work Clearances Permit Governance

Digital Permit to Work system managing Hot Work, Confined Space Entry, High-Voltage Switching, and Cold Work. Tightly integrated with S/4HANA Work Clearance Management (WCM) and maintenance orders, preventing technician execution until all permits are electronically counter-signed.

Digital PTW Workflows Confined Space / Hot Work WCM Isolation Gates
4. Management of Change (MOC) & Environmental Emissions Change & Environment

Structured Management of Change (MOC) governance ensuring multi-disciplinary reviews and Pre-Startup Safety Reviews (PSSR) before commissioning modified plant assets. Environmental module calculates greenhouse gas emissions (GHG Scope 1 & 2) and monitors wastewater limits.

MOC & PSSR Approval GHG Scope 1/2 Tracking Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

// Electronic Permit to Work interlock for SSAM execution
export default async function ValidatePermitToWork(context) {
  const orderId = context.getBindingObject().OrderId;
  const permitStatus = await context.read(
    '/Services/EHSM.service',
    `WorkPermits(OrderId='${orderId}',Type='HOT_WORK')`
  );

  if (!permitStatus.IsAuthorized || permitStatus.AtmosphericGasPPM > 0) {
    // Abort execution if gas reading is unsafe
    await context.executeAction(
      '/Actions/SafetyLockoutModal.action'
    );
    return false;
  }
  
  return context.executeAction(
    '/Actions/TransitionToExecutionPhase.action'
  );
}
