SAP Service and Asset Manager (SSAM) & Field Service Management (FSM)

Offline-first field execution, sub-second delta synchronization, and dynamic maintenance inspection forms.

Consult Mobility Architects

In demanding field environments—from offshore drilling rigs and substations to sprawling factory floors—cellular coverage is intermittent or nonexistent. We design and implement mission-critical mobile solutions built on SAP Service and Asset Manager (SSAM 2310/2405) and SAP Field Service Management (FSM). Our architectures guarantee 100% offline operational continuity, deterministic delta queue resolution, and zero asset telemetry loss across thousands of concurrent field technicians.
