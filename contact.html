Client Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is covered in a LAN Innovations Architecture Review?

We evaluate your existing mobile deployment, delta synchronization logs, MAIF filtering overhead, SAP BTP Integration Suite setup, and S/4HANA clean core alignment. You receive a concrete technical scorecard and implementation blueprint.

Do you work with on-premise SAP ECC landscapes, or only S/4HANA?

Both. We engineer modern SSAM and MDK solutions connecting either directly to SAP ECC via SAP Gateway/MAIF or to modern S/4HANA Cloud and On-Premise using ABAP RAP and SAP BTP.

How are enterprise security, IP, and NDAs managed?

We execute mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) prior to any code or architecture inspection. All work is performed in compliance with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and enterprise security policies.

What is your team structure during implementation?

We deploy lean pods consisting of principal SAP mobility architects and senior full-stack AI engineers. You communicate directly with the builders writing your production code—no junior outsourcing, no multi-layer middle management.
